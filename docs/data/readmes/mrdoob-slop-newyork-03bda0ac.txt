Slop New York

A New York City simulation built with three.js, WebGPURenderer, and TSL. Explore Manhattan on foot, by car, by bicycle, or by helicopter.

Try it live

Make it your own

Fork it, remix it, improve it, break it. Make games, animations, films, experiments, or something completely different. Use it as a starting point and take it wherever you want. Have fun!

Explore other cities and community remixes in Slop Cities.
