Why TreasureHourAlert

A Pirate Shop Token found by anyone in a squad unlocks for all four. So the best plan is four people opening chests in four different places, not four people looting one town together.

| | Landing together | TreasureHourAlert |
|---|---|---|
| Chests a match | About 17, shared between four | About 31, one corner each |
| Who goes where | Shouted in voice chat | Routes from the bus line, sent to the squad |
| Treasure Hour | Found out too late | Alert in your time zone, 15 minutes before |
| Your odds | Guesswork and rumors | Plain math on chests you actually opened |
| Your account | Shared with "boosting" services | Never leaves your PC |

What you get

Squad split

Draw the bus line and TreasureHourAlert gives each squadmate a corner of the island with a route through it. Hot drops are avoided, and routes are rebuilt from the chests your squad actually found there. One click sends the plan to everyone.

Honest odds

TreasureHourAlert counts every chest the squad opens and turns it into a chance you can read: how likely the next chest is, how many matches a 5% chance takes. The more your squad counts, the better the math gets.

Treasure Hour alerts

Every Treasure Hour in your own time zone, with an alert for the whole squad and a calendar file. The scam check explains the shortcuts people sell around the token, and why each one costs you your account.

Download and start TreasureHourAlert.exe.

TreasureHourAlert.exe is code-signed, so Windows starts it without a warning. Keep the files together: they ship as one folder.

How it works

1. Count chests with a hotkey, or let TreasureHourAlert read the replay files Fortnite saves on your PC after each match.
2. Plan the drop: TreasureHourAlert gets the current map and place names from fortnite-api.com, and you draw the bus line.
3. Share the plan with a link or your squad's Discord channel.

TreasureHourAlert never injects anything, never reads game memory, and never asks for your Epic login.

FAQ

Will I get banned?
TreasureHourAlert doesn't touch the game, its files or its memory. It counts chests from a hotkey and the replays the game already saves, and plans happen outside the match.

Is this made by Epic?
No. TreasureHourAlert is an independent fan project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games.

Will it cost money?
No. Epic's Fan Content Policy only allows free fan apps, so TreasureHourAlert stays $0 with no ads and no paid tier.
