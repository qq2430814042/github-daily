Price model: per second, not per task

Seedance 2.5 Flash bills by output second, so clip length is the main cost lever.

Where the money actually goes

- Iteration is the cost, not the final render — draft at 480p, finish at 720p
- Reference-video requests are billed at a separate (higher) rate
- Failed tasks are refunded automatically
