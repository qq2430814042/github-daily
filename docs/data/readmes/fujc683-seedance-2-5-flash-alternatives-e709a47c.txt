What to compare

| Need | Pick |
|---|---|
| Fast, cheap iteration | Seedance 2.5 Flash (480p) |
| Long clips (up to 30 s) | Seedance 2.5 Flash |
| Best motion realism | Veo 3 |
| Stylized / anime motion | Kling 4.0 |
| Audio + video together | Veo 3 |
| Image-to-video from a still | Seedance 2.5 Flash with reference_image |

When Seedance 2.5 Flash is the wrong pick

- You need 1080p → Veo 3
- You need native audio → Veo 3
- You need 4K stills → use an image model instead
