✨ New: the story check

Clips used to be picked from the sound alone. Sometimes the hook promised something that never
showed up, or the cut stopped one second before the punchline. Now your AI looks at the frames
of every moment before it cuts:

- 🎬 The whole scene. Each clip starts 3-10 seconds before the crazy thing happens and ends right after the streamer's reaction.
- 🎯 Hooks that match the picture. If the hook says something the video doesn't show, it gets rewritten. If nothing worth posting is on screen, a backup moment takes its place.
- 🔥 Reaction first. It ranks moments by how hard the streamer loses it (screaming, laughing, jumping up), then makes sure you see what caused it.
- 💬 Real captions. What the streamer says stays as it is. Only slurs are starred out in the on-screen text (TikTok and Reels flag them and bury the post). The audio is never changed.

Works the same in Claude and ChatGPT (and Kimi, Gemini or no AI at all). Nothing new to learn: you ask for clips exactly like before.

🧠 Tips that make clips go viral
- Post fast. The first clips of a new moment get the views. This tool exists for speed.
- The hook decides everything. People decide in one second. Every reel comes with a hook plus 2 extra hooks to A/B test.
- Add a trending sound inside the app at low volume (YouTube: music lowers the Shorts payout pool, so use it on TikTok and Reels).
- Post daily, 3-5 reels, on every platform the campaign allows.
- Pick streamers with clipping programs and follow their campaign rules exactly.

🛠️ How it works

1. You say "Make GTA 6 clips" to Claude or ChatGPT.
2. It asks three questions:
   - How many clips? (default 4)
   - Streams from the last 24 hours, 48 hours or 1 week? (default 48 hours)
   - Which style? It shows you the five styles. Pick one, several, or all. (default: Hook card)
3. It shows you the most viewed streams with views and age. Pick some, or say "take the top ones".
4. It does the work (about 10-20 minutes per stream) and tells you what it's doing.
5. You get a folder of finished reels plus a POSTING.md with the caption and hashtags for each one.
6. You post them to the campaign and collect.

| Step | What happens behind the scenes |
|---|---|
| 🔎 Finds the streams | Searches YouTube for the most viewed GTA 6 streams in your time window, skips re-uploads and compilations, checks the language. |
| 👀 Watches the whole stream | Looks at the entire stream in seconds and skips waiting rooms, menus and just chatting. Only real gameplay. |
| 👂 Listens for the hype | Finds the moments where the streamer screams, laughs and chat explodes. |
| 🧠 Picks the viral moments | Your AI chooses the funniest, most shocking moments and writes a hook for each. |
| ✂️ Cuts only what it needs | Downloads only those seconds of video (plus a little extra on each side), not the whole 10 hours. |
| 🎞️ Checks the story | Looks at the frames and re-cuts each moment so it shows the whole scene, from just before the surprise to the end of the reaction. Rewrites a hook that doesn't match the picture. |
| 🎬 Edits like a pro | 9:16, hook text, word-by-word captions, zooms and sound effects in 5 styles. |
| ✅ Checks its own work | Looks at every finished reel before handing it to you. |
